# Playtest notes

No external player playtests have been claimed. Automated rule and flow checks are recorded in `progress.md`.

## Suggested first-session questions

- What did you think would happen when you advanced the turn?
- Which council choice felt hardest, and why?
- Did scouting change your battlefield plan?
- What caused your death or victory?
- After a rewind, what did you plan to change?
- Did you discover both the evidence-led and defence-led endings?
